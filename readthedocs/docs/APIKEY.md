# APIKEY

One of the main features of cloudHPC is its integration with third-party software. This is made possible by your APIKEY, a unique identifier that lets you interact with cloudHPC through API calls.

Every account is assigned its own APIKEY when it is created. Third-party software needs this APIKEY to link to your account.

!!! note
    The API may not be available on the FREE plan (i.e. while using the initial 300 vCPU Hours).

## Retrieving your APIKEY
Your APIKEY is shown on your profile page. To open it, click "profile" under the "account" menu, as shown in the following image:

<p align="center">
   <img width="800" src="https://cfdfeaservice.it/wiki/cloud-hpc/images/ProfilePage.jpg">
</p>

On your account details page, your APIKEY is the string shown in the corresponding box.

<p align="center">
   <img width="600" src="https://cfdfeaservice.it/wiki/cloud-hpc/images/Apikey.jpg">
</p>

!!! danger
    Store your APIKEY safely and, if in doubt, contact us at info@cfdfeaservice.it. Sharing your APIKEY with someone gives them full access to your account: they can see your simulations and results, and use your account.
